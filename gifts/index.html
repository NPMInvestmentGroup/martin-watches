Logo, personalized & group orders

How Custom Logo & Group Orders Work

  1. Contact usRequest a design meeting or call 281-838-5684. Tell us the occasion, how many watches, and the names, initials or logo you want. Corporate and group customers work with us directly on every order.
  2. Send your logoA simple image of your logo is all we need. No special file format is required, and we digitize the artwork for you.
  3. Approve your mockupEvery build gets a digital mockup for your approval before production. Names, initials and logos can go on the dial, with engraving available on the caseback and in other locations.
  4. Invoice & paymentWe send an invoice, and you can pay by credit card, cash or wire transfer, among other accepted methods. Group orders cost the same per watch, with no minimum.
  5. Hand-assembled in CrosbyStandard production is typically 4–6 weeks. For weddings, retirements and corporate events, start 6–8 weeks before the date. Need it sooner? Expedited builds may be possible when the parts are in stock, sometimes as soon as the next day, but this isn’t guaranteed.
  6. Delivered ready to giveEach watch ships in its own Martin Watch Co. presentation case with a one-year limited warranty and free shipping anywhere in the U.S.
