About Martin Watch Co.

Made by hand.
Designed by you.

I've always admired fine watches, and I've always known that most people never get to own one.

In 2025 I built my first watch myself. Learning what goes into every dial, hand and movement changed how I saw them, and it gave me an idea: what if more people could own a watch that was not only well made, but designed by them?

That idea became Martin Watch Co. Every watch is built by me, by hand, in Crosby, Texas.

More than a watch.

When you buy a Martin watch, you're buying an experience. We talk through your design together, trading ideas back and forth until every detail is right. What you wear at the end is exactly what you wanted, and something you had a real hand in creating.

— Quanah (Trey) Martin, Founder
